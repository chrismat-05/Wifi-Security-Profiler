What we inspect?

Core telemetry dimensions analyzed continuously to classify wireless risks.

⊙

Hardware Telemetry

Directly queries Windows netsh interfaces for real-time SSID, BSSID MAC addresses, radio bands, and signal quality.

✶

Captive Portals

Probes outbound HTTP gateways to identify unencrypted internal splash pages and intercepted credential login portals.

⚠

Cipher & WPS

Evaluates cryptographic suites to flag deprecated WEP keys, broken TKIP ciphers, and WPS brute-force vectors.

◆

Risk Grading

Calculates an objective 0–100 penalty score, stratifies risk into 4 tiers, and generates actionable hardening steps.
