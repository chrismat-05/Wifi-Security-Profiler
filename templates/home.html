About Wi-Fi Security Profiler

Wireless local area networks (WLANs) represent one of the primary entry points for cyber attacks, ranging from unauthorized eavesdropping and evil twin rogue access points to unencrypted credential interception over captive portals. Modern enterprise and personal computing environments require proactive assessment of wireless protocol configurations to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and regulatory compliance.

This profiling system bridges the gap between complex packet inspection utilities and everyday operational audits. By querying system hardware interfaces, executing lightweight heuristic probes against standard captive portal triggers, and running rule-based pattern matching, Wi-Fi Security Profiler delivers instant visibility into whether a wireless connection satisfies modern cybersecurity standards or introduces severe organizational risks.

Core Capabilities & Security Checks

01

Hardware Interface Detection

Automatically inspects the local wireless adapter via operating system utilities to extract active connection parameters including SSID, BSSID, cipher suites, channel radio standards, and real-time signal quality.

02

Captive Portal & Web Login Probing

Probes network connectivity endpoints to detect internal redirection gateways and splash pages. Identifies unencrypted authentication portals that expose student, employee, or guest credentials to man-in-the-middle interception.

03

Nearby Access Point Discovery

Scans over-the-air frequencies to discover all visible wireless access points in range. Profiles each visible beacon to detect rogue open networks and insecure enterprise configurations.

04

Rule-Based Vulnerability Engine

Evaluates network parameters against a comprehensive cybersecurity vulnerability rule set, detecting obsolete WEP keys, broken TKIP ciphers, WPS PIN brute-force exposures, and weak authentication passwords.

05

Quantitative Risk Scoring

Computes an objective risk score ranging from 0 (Extremely Secure) to 100 (Critical Vulnerability), stratifying evaluated networks into CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW risk categories.

06

Actionable Remediation Reports

Generates explicit, actionable remediation protocols for each evaluated network, advising administrators and end users on cipher migration, VPN tunneling, and Wi-Fi Protected Access (WPA3) implementation.

Audit Methodology & Workflow

STEP 1
Telemetry Acquisition
The engine queries wireless hardware layers and probes outbound gateway routes to extract cryptographic settings and detect internal captive portal redirection.
STEP 2
Vulnerability Mapping
The rule engine cross-references acquired specs against known wireless attack vectors including KRACK, WPS Pixie Dust, dictionary attacks, and cleartext login interception.
STEP 3
Profile Evaluation
Risk penalties are computed and synthesized into persistent profiles, rendering interactive cards with prioritized hardening recommendations.
